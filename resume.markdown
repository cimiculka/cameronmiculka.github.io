                <p>Cameron Miculka is a journalist in Kailua-Kona, Hawai‘i.<br />
                Since starting his career in 2008 with The Weimar Mercury, a weekly print publication in Weimar, Texas, Cameron has covered a variety of beats, including public safety, education and infrastructure.<br />
                Whether featuring one man's bid to sail from Taiwan to Guam in a Chamorro proa for the <a href = "https://www.guampdn.com">Pacific Daily News</a>, or illuminating the "behind-closed-doors" epidemic through a four-part series on the opioid crisis for <a href = "https://www.westhawaiitoday.com">West Hawaii Today</a>, Cameron's work has highlighted the challenges residents face in small, rural communities of Texas, Guam and Hawai‘i as well as celebrated the tight bonds formed within them.</p>
