Cameron Miculka is a journalist in Kailua-Kona, Hawai‘i.
Since starting his journalism career in 2008 with The Weimar Mercury, a weekly print publication in Weimar, Texas, Cameron has covered a variety of beats, including public safety, education and infrastructure.
Whether featuring one man's bid to sail from Taiwan to Guam in a Chamorro proa for the [Pacific Daily News](https://www.guampdn.com), or illuminating the "behind-closed-doors" epidemic through a four-part series on the opioid crisis for [West Hawaii Today](https://www.westhawaiitoday.com), Cameron's work has highlighted the challenges residents face in small, rural communities of Texas, Guam and Hawai‘i as well as celebrated the tight bonds formed within them.
